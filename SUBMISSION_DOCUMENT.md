# UniPath LK — submission description

UniPath LK helps Sri Lankan post-A/L students explore education routes across 12 fields using qualifications, goals, budget and study preferences. Its small researched catalogue contains 37 programmes from seven providers, including practical vocational options. Each result exposes evidence, unknowns, conditions and estimated cash flow rather than inventing eligibility, fees or guarantees.

Jac nodes, edges and request-local walkers explore bounded routes. A typed byLLM Gemini planner selects allowlisted actions; deterministic rules remain authoritative. The study guide uses typed programme/tip choices and server-owned explanations. Another-A/L-sitting scenario compares estimated preparation spending and time with current routes; official admissions policy is labelled by year and scope.

Native Jac client: profile, routes, side-by-side comparison, evidence, graph/activity, retake scenario, Colombo clock and optional study guide. Local verification: 18 checked Jac modules, 54 native tests, ten explicit HTTP groups, successful native client build and one real live_ai guide call. Owner confirms both new panels open; full current browser review remains incomplete.
