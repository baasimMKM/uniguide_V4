# Catalogue evidence review

Checked on 2026-10-17. This is a curated discovery sample of 31 programmes in 12 fields, not a complete Sri Lankan admissions service. All 28 baseline records remain; an OUSL English diploma plus direct post-A/L discovery records for OUSL Psychology and University of Colombo MBBS were added. Each field has at least two records. Institutions represented are OUSL, University of Colombo, Sabaragamuwa University, SLIATE and SLIIT.

## What verified means

Each claim records an exact official source URL, check date, status and a narrow paraphrased note. Programme identity only establishes that an institution publishes the programme. It does not establish an open 2026 intake, professional accreditation, admission eligibility, affordability or employment outcomes. Current intake stays unreviewed. Entry conditions observed on undated or non-current-intake material stay unreviewed; dated 2026 pages can verify the published conditions while their future intake remains unknown.

Research used the initial official-page identity/admission evidence and a manual review of programme-specific institutional pages. There is no live scraping or external website dependency in product operation. Full page quotations are not stored in this catalogue.

## Admission and progression limits

Rules model the known direct Sri Lankan A/L conditions and exact supported catalogue/credential alternatives. Unmodelled alternative/equivalence routes explicitly retain institutional confirmation rather than falsely rejecting them. A/L sitting, age, credit grades, faculty selection, detailed subject combinations and recognised equivalences may be outside the profile schema and must be checked by the institution. This is why passing the encoded conditions never guarantees entry.

The external qualifications list contains source-named prior qualifications/study milestones; these are not invented course offerings or promises of recognition. Nursing, Special Needs Education, Drama and Theatre, and Zoology Honours require prior qualifications/study. Medical Laboratory Sciences explicitly permits direct science A/L entry with Chemistry; its diploma provision concerns exemptions.

The single real edge is OUSL Diploma in English Language and Literature → BA English and English Language Teaching. The BA page explicitly accepts that named diploma and exempts that entry category from the selection test; an application and admission decision remain required. The route retains the full published BA duration and makes no credit-transfer, shortened-duration or guaranteed-entry claim. [Diploma source](https://ou.ac.lk/programme/diploma-in-english/) and [BA admission source](https://ou.ac.lk/programme/bachelor-of-arts-honours-in-english-and-english-language-teaching/).

Zoology Honours is a within-programme 2+2 selection after BSc Levels 3/4 with grades/GPA, so no false completed-BSc → Honours edge was added. Its published four years covers the whole award; remaining duration after prior study is left null. Other institutions’ progression marketing was not used to invent degree transfers.

## Cost, duration and delivery

All upfront, monthly and travel costs remain null. Published application fees, per-credit charges and indicative totals do not establish complete cash-flow schedules. A few dated published fee components have separate evidence claims; the complete-fees claim stays unreviewed. Diploma English displays a total inconsistent with the sum of its components, so no numeric budget estimate was inferred. Special Needs Education fee figures also appear inconsistent and remain unreviewed.

Civil Engineering is corrected to the selected programme’s four-year minimum, Management Studies to four years, Youth and Community Development to three years, and HNDIT to 30 months. Mechanical Engineering has conflicting four-/five-year statements on its own official page, so duration is null/stale. Nursing’s two-year value refers only to the prior-diploma Level 5/6 route; the page separately calls the full degree four years. Unchecked durations are null. English BA explicitly combines online and onsite attendance; every other delivery mode stays unknown pending programme-specific confirmation.

## Editorial goal-search vocabulary

Each of the 31 records now includes goal_tags for subject exploration. The terms come from programme titles or published curricular subjects; role words such as nurse, accountant, lawyer, psychologist and doctor are search vocabulary, not verified professional pathways. They do not establish admission, career suitability, professional recognition, registration or employment. General management tags are distinct from accounting/accountancy/finance tags; tourism and hospitality are also differentiated. The Diploma English tags describe English/literature, not a standalone teaching qualification. Tags may affect discovery order only after hard eligibility/evidence status and may not override requirements, unknown costs or selection conditions. No source claim was upgraded by adding these editorial associations.
