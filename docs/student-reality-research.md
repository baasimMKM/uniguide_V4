# Student reality research — repeating A/Ls and practical alternatives

Checked 17 October 2026. This note records primary-source evidence and a read-only logic review. It does not determine an individual’s admission, right to sit an examination or likelihood of improving grades.

## Official guidance and its scope

The [UGC 2025/2026 admissions handbook](https://www.ugc.ac.lk/downloads/admissions/Handbook_2025_26/student_handbook_english.pdf) was downloaded from UGC and its relevant pages extracted locally. Section 1.2, printed p. 9 (PDF p. 16), requires three approved subject passes in one sitting within the permitted three attempts, and Common General Paper marks of at least 30%. Its stated earlier-result provision concerns that paper; it does not permit combining main-subject passes across sittings. Minimum requirements remain distinct from competitive selection and course prerequisites.

Section 1.7, printed p. 13 (PDF p. 20), addresses internal admission under the handbook and excludes more-than-three examination occasions. Attempts need not be consecutive. A student considering another sitting should ask UGC how this affects their actual admission history and any earlier result. These are rules for the stated academic year and scope, rather than a universal examination-sitting ban.

The [Department of Examinations 2021 private-candidate instructions](https://doenets.lk/images/resources/NEWS/AL%20paper%20add%202021%20English%20-%201_1625816833750.pdf) distinguish non-appearance and school/private-candidate categories. They are explicitly historical here; their application dates, fees and personal-attempt treatment are not presented as current. Check the next examination’s actual notice through the [official DoE portal](https://onlineexams.gov.lk/). The checked portal did not establish a future retake application window.

## Product review and limits

The read-only review of `services/retake.jac` found no automatic ineligibility state or examination ban. Three-or-more self-reported sittings produce a year-scoped warning and referral; the service still returns routes from the ordinary deterministic planner. It does not calculate future grades, an admission probability, a guaranteed income or a retake fee. Unknown preparation estimates remain incomplete. Income forgone is shown separately from expenses, rather than treated as money the family certainly would have earned.

Students supply their preparation months and recurring/one-off costs, including travel and materials. Study hours, work, caring duties, family support and relocation are discussion inputs rather than admission facts. Compare the waiting and preparation period with actual course schedules, local availability and household commitments; the product cannot settle those trade-offs from marks alone.

The new increment has no independent browser QA from this review: browser control is disabled by the current Work setting. Earlier screenshots, video and scoped browser results predate the retake interface. Test execution is owned by the main verification task; this review makes no new test-count claim.
